# HV1V2 deployment bundle — run `v6` (decoupled)

The policy drives **only the 12 leg joints**. The other 19 joints belong to
your manipulation/IK controller: waist yaw, shoulders, elbows, wrists and head,
plus waist roll/pitch, which must stay **locked at home**. The legs were
trained to stay put while that upper body moves, carries up to 2 kg per hand
and takes hand forces.

## Who drives what

| | joints | source of the position target |
|---|---|---|
| **policy** | 12: `right_hip_pitch` … `right_ankle_roll` (0–5), `left_hip_pitch` … `left_ankle_roll` (6–11) | `anchor + raw * action.scale` |
| **IK** | 17: `waist_yaw`, shoulders ×6, elbows ×2, wrists ×6, head ×2 | your IK controller, inside `upper_body.trained_range` |
| **IK, locked** | 2: `waist_roll`, `waist_pitch` | hold `upper_body.home` (0.0) — **always** |

All 31 joints still run the same joint-space PD (`gains` in `deploy.json`). The
split is only about **who writes each target**. Every target must be written
every cycle. **With no IK running, write `upper_body.home`**: never leave the
upper-body targets unwritten or at zero. In sim, a zero target drives the
elbows from 0.9 to 0 rad.

## Control loop

```
control_dt = 0.020 s   (50 Hz)     sim_timestep 0.005, decimation 4
root_body  = pelvis
```

Each control step:

1. assemble the 403-wide observation (below);
2. `raw = policy(obs)` → **12** values;
3. for `k` in 0..11: `j = action.joint_indices[k]`,
   `target[j] = anchor[j] + raw[k] * action.scale[action.joint_names[k]]`;
4. for every upper-body joint: `target[j] = IK output`, clamped to
   `upper_body.trained_range` and rate-limited to 2.0 rad/s. Waist roll/pitch
   stay at home;
5. write all 31 targets to the joint PD controllers, in `deploy.json` joint order;
6. keep `raw` (12) — it is the `actions` observation term next step.

## Observation — 403, term-major, oldest first

| term | dim | history | contributes | source on robot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `base_ang_vel` | 3 | 5 | 15 | IMU angular velocity, **pelvis frame**, rad/s |
| `projected_gravity` | 3 | 5 | 15 | `R_pelvis^T @ [0,0,-1]` — a **unit** vector, not 9.81 |
| `joint_pos` | **31** | 5 | 155 | `q - anchor` — **all 31 joints, upper body included** |
| `joint_vel` | **31** | 5 | 155 | `qdot`, all 31 |
| `actions` | **12** | 5 | 60 | previous **raw** policy output (12), before `action.scale` |
| `command` | 3 | 1 | 3 | operator `[vx, vy, wz]` |

The policy reads the **measured** upper-body joint state, not the IK targets:
that state is how it sees the disturbance coming. So the encoders of the IK
joints must be in the observation even though the policy never commands them.

**Layout — wrong ordering does not error, it silently feeds garbage.** Each
term's whole history is contiguous, *then* terms concatenate in the order
above. The **last** `dim` values of each slice are the current reading:

```
[ang_vel t-4..t0][gravity t-4..t0][joint_pos t-4..t0][joint_vel t-4..t0][actions t-4..t0][command t0]
```

**At t=0, backfill all 5 slots with the first reading.** Never zero-fill.

## Upper-body contract: what the legs were trained to tolerate

| | trained |
|---|---|
| joint range | `upper_body.trained_range`, per joint (see `_joint_table.md`) |
| max target slew | **2.0 rad/s** |
| payload | **0–2 kg** at each hand (`left/right_wrist_yaw_link`) |

| waist roll/pitch | **locked at 0**, never moved in training |

Measured in sim2sim, standing still (command 0) with 2 kg in each hand:

| upper-body motion | foot drift | steps | max pelvis tilt |
|---|---|---|---|
| held at home | 3–5 mm | 0 | 0.8° |
| random, full trained envelope | 0.4–4 mm | 0 | 3.0° |

## READY pose

Servo all 31 joints to the anchor (`ready_pose` in `deploy.json`) with joint
PD, start the IK side holding `upper_body.home`, then hand the legs to the
policy. The pose was solved with the CoM over the sole centre (**0.41 mm** off,
feet flat, stance 0.24304 m, pelvis 0.97743 m).

> **It does not stand open-loop.** Holding it with PD and no policy, the robot
> topples in ~3.8 s — ankle stiffness is ~95% of the `m·g·h` the inverted
> pendulum demands. Keep the policy running.

Left/right leg values differ (`right_knee` 0.376 vs `left` 0.324). That is
**correct**: the hip-pitch axes are canted ~30° and the legs are not mirror
images.

## Four-bar joints — READ BEFORE WRITING GAINS

`left/right_ankle_pitch`, `left/right_ankle_roll` (policy) and `waist_roll`,
`waist_pitch` (locked) are parallel four-bars driven by **two** X8-120s each.
The linkage **amplifies** commanded joint gain, so writing the simulation
`kp = 400` straight to the motors will over-stiffen them. Measure the
amplification on HV1V2 and divide first. H1-V1's factors (`ankle_pitch 2.41x,
ankle_roll 1.23x, waist_roll 4.00x, waist_pitch 4.81x`) were measured **on
H1-V1**, a starting point for a measurement, not values. `waist_yaw` is a
passthrough.

## Known limits

- **Foot clearance is low**: ~6–7 mm at 1.0 m/s, only **1.5–3 mm** at slow
  walking, sideways and turning. That is close to sliding the feet, so expect
  little tolerance for floor irregularity. Test on a clean, flat floor at low
  commands, with a gantry.
- **Fast cadence**: ~3.6 steps/s at 1.0 m/s. Check the leg actuators keep up
  on hardware before going above ~0.6 m/s.
- **Sim-only disturbance model**: payload is a point mass at the wrist link,
  and hand force is a constant wrench. Real contact (pushing a table, a
  grasped object swinging) is not modelled.
- **`armature` is provisional**: vendor `J·ratio²`, hedged with DR `(0.6, 1.4)`.
- **No thermal model.** Watch `effort_continuous_nm`.
