## Control loop

```
control_dt = 0.020 s   (50 Hz)     sim_timestep 0.005, decimation 4
root_body  = pelvis
```

Each control step:

1. assemble the 498-wide observation (below);
2. `raw = policy(obs)` → 31 values;
3. `target[i] = anchor[i] + raw[i] * action_scale[i]`;
4. write `target` to the joint PD controllers, in the joint order in `deploy.json`;
5. keep `raw` — it is the `actions` observation term next step.

## Observation — 498, term-major, oldest first

| term | dim | history | contributes | source on robot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `base_ang_vel` | 3 | 5 | 15 | IMU angular velocity, **pelvis frame**, rad/s |
| `projected_gravity` | 3 | 5 | 15 | `R_pelvis^T @ [0,0,-1]` — a **unit** vector, not 9.81 |
| `joint_pos` | 31 | 5 | 155 | `q - anchor` |
| `joint_vel` | 31 | 5 | 155 | `qdot` |
| `actions` | 31 | 5 | 155 | previous **raw** output, before `action_scale` |
| `command` | 3 | 1 | 3 | operator `[vx, vy, wz]` |

**Layout — wrong ordering does not error, it silently feeds garbage.** Each
term's whole history is contiguous, *then* terms concatenate in the order
above. Oldest frame first, so the **last** `dim` values of each slice are the
current reading:

```
[ang_vel t-4..t0][gravity t-4..t0][joint_pos t-4..t0][joint_vel t-4..t0][actions t-4..t0][command t0]
```

This is **term-major**. Many stacks are frame-major — if yours is, reindex.

**At t=0, backfill all 5 slots with the first reading.** Do not zero-fill; the
policy never saw zeros during training and the first 5 steps would be
off-distribution.

## READY pose

Servo here with joint PD, then hand over to the policy. It is the same as the
anchor — safe because the pose was solved with the CoM over the sole centre
(**0.41 mm** off, feet flat, stance 0.24304 m, pelvis 0.97743 m).

> **It does not stand open-loop.** Holding it with PD and no policy, the robot
> topples in ~3.8 s — ankle stiffness is ~95% of the `m·g·h` the inverted
> pendulum demands (841 N·m/rad needed, 800 supplied). The policy must be
> running. Do not use READY as a resting state.

Note the left/right leg values differ (`right_knee` 0.376 vs `left` 0.324).
That is **correct** — the hip-pitch axes are canted ~30° and the legs are not
mirror images. Symmetrising the pose tilts the feet.

## What changed from v4

v5 is v4 (`model_39999.pt`) fine-tuned for 8000 iterations towards **shorter
steps, shorter swings and quiet arms**, with the waist still locked. The
interface is **identical to v4**: same 498 inputs, 31 outputs, joint order, anchor,
gains, action scales and pinned set. A runtime that runs v4 runs v5 by swapping
`policy.onnx` and **tightening the command limits below**.

Measured in sim2sim at fixed commands (v4 → v5):

| command | step length | swing time | cadence | arm RMS speed | foot lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vx 0.3 | 0.144 → 0.087 m | 0.53 → 0.28 s | 1.71 → 2.93 /s | 0.34 → 0.14 rad/s | 3.2 → 1.3 mm |
| vx 0.6 | 0.246 → 0.204 m | 0.42 → 0.30 s | 2.21 → 2.86 /s | 0.56 → 0.18 rad/s | 4.8 → 4.7 mm |
| vx 1.0 | 0.395 → 0.316 m | 0.35 → 0.23 s | 2.57 → 3.79 /s | 0.80 → 0.30 rad/s | 8.7 → 6.1 mm |

| wz 0.5 | — | 0.50 → 0.29 s | 1.86 → 2.93 /s | 0.32 → 0.12 rad/s | 2.5 → 1.2 mm |

## Commands

`[vx, vy, wz]`, trained ranges only. **These are narrower than v4's. Clamp to
them in the runtime.**

| | min | max | v4 was |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lin_vel_x` | −0.8 | 1.0 | −1.2 .. 1.5 |

## Four-bar joints — READ BEFORE WRITING GAINS

Six joints are parallel four-bars driven by **two** X8-120s each:

```
left/right_ankle_pitch, left/right_ankle_roll, waist_roll, waist_pitch
```

The linkage **amplifies** commanded joint gain, so writing the simulation
`kp = 400` straight to the motors will over-stiffen them. Measure the
amplification on HV1V2 and divide first.

H1-V1 measured `ankle_pitch 2.41x, ankle_roll 1.23x, waist_roll 4.00x,
waist_pitch 4.81x` — **on H1-V1 hardware, not this robot**. Use those as a
starting point for a measurement, not as values.

`waist_yaw` is a passthrough and needs no correction.

## Pinned joints

11 of 31 have `action_scale = 0.0`: the three waist, six wrist, two head. The
policy cannot move them, so they hold the anchor. Their raw outputs are
unpriced and drift freely — **if you ever un-pin one, add a cost in the same
change or it will slam**.

Live: 12 legs, 6 shoulders, 2 elbows.

## Known limits

- **Foot clearance is the main risk: worse than v4.** About 6 mm at 1.0 m/s,
  and only **1.2–1.6 mm at slow walking, sideways stepping and turning**,
  against an 80 mm training target. That is close to sliding the feet. Any
  floor irregularity (a mat edge, a cable, a seam) can catch a foot. Test on a
  clean, flat floor first, at low commands, with a gantry.
- **Fast cadence.** About 3.8 steps/s at 1.0 m/s. Check that the leg actuators'
  tracking bandwidth keeps up on hardware before going above about 0.6 m/s.
- **Torque** worst actuator averages ~129% of continuous rating over training
  episodes (v4: 156%), with 3.6% of actuator-steps above continuous (v4: 5.5%) — inside the
  vendor's 10 s stall rating, but log it. `effort_peak_nm` in `deploy.json` is
  the vendor PEAK (a 3–10 s rating), `effort_continuous_nm` is what the motor
  actually sustains.
- **`armature` is provisional** — vendor `J·ratio²`, which assumes the
  published inertia is motor-side; the vendor never states it. Trained with
  domain randomisation over `(0.6, 1.4)` to hedge.
- **No thermal model exists.** Watch `effort_continuous_nm`.
